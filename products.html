Surface Finish Dynamics & Thickness Anatomy

Matte vs Gloss Finish

Matte vs Gloss Reflection

Matte: Zero glare, anti-fingerprint silky touch. Ideal for expansive wardrobe shutters and minimalist kitchen faces.

Gloss: Deep 92%+ reflectivity with optical depth. Creates high-impact accent shutters and premium retail display fronts.

Calibrated Thickness Anatomy

Architectural Thickness Guide

  • 6–9mm: Drawer bottoms, wall panel backing, lightweight curved partitions.
  • 12mm: Internal carcasses, partition shelving, lightweight cabinetry.
  • 18–19mm: Structural kitchen carcasses, wardrobe shutters, heavy load worktops.
