About STRATA · Surface Decor & Structural Panels

Our Philosophy · Surface Matters

Bridging High-Design Aesthetics With Industrial Precision

Inspired by India's premier surface decor pioneers, STRATA PLY & LAMINATES was founded with a singular mission: to give architects, interior designers, modular furniture factories, and turnkey contractors a unified ecosystem where decorative surfaces and structural cores work in complete harmony.

Instead of sourcing plywood from one vendor, decorative laminates or acrylic sheets from another, and hunting for close-enough edge bands on site, our clients access a fully cross-matched catalog — backed by batch-tested IS:710 calibrated plywood and 98%+ shade-matched PVC edge banding.

Manufacturing & Sourcing Standards

What Sets Our Panels Apart

01

European Design Papers & Registered Emboss

Our 0.8mm, 1.0mm (Canvas), and 1.25mm (Design Yatra) high-pressure laminates utilize high-grammage imported decor papers pressed with stainless steel synchronized texture plates — delivering authentic timber pores, stone clefts, and anti-fingerprint matte surfaces.

02

Zero-Gap Calibrated Hardwood Cores

Every 710 BWP, BWR, and MR board is composed of high-density Gurjan and Eucalyptus core veneers, machine-composed and two-stage calibrated to a strict ±0.1mm tolerance so CNC routers and automated edge-banders run without chatter or telegraphing.

03

Integrated Edge-to-Surface Ecosystem

A luxury laminate or 6H acrylic shutter is only as refined as its edge. We stock precision-extruded PVC and ABS edge bands in super-matte, mirror-gloss, and woodgrain textures engineered to disappear against the panel face.
