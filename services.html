What We Do

Specialized Surface & Plywood Services

Modelled on India's leading surface decor brands, our service ecosystem eliminates friction between architectural specification, modular factory processing, and on-site installation.

01 · Lookbook & Swatch Dispatch

Physical A4 laminate sheets, 6H acrylic chips, and master folders (Canvas, Design Yatra, Vibron) couriered to architecture studios for client moodboards.

  • 48-hour express studio courier
  • Full A4 & chain-set swatch binders
  • High-res BIM / SketchUp seamless textures

02 · 98%+ Edge Band Matching

Zero dark-line joints. Every decorative laminate and acrylic sheet in our catalog comes pre-mapped to its exact PVC/ABS edge band roll in matching gloss or emboss.

  • Sizes: 22×0.8mm, 22×1.3mm, 22×2.0mm & wide 45mm
  • Super-matte, mirror-gloss & woodgrain emboss
  • Slit-to-order roll quantities for OEMs

03 · Calibrated & CNC-Ready Plywood

Two-stage sanded calibrated plywood (6mm to 19mm) with ±0.1mm thickness uniformity — built for high-speed German/Italian beam saws and cold/hot pressing lines.

  • IS:710 BWP, IS:303 BWR & MR grades
  • Warp-free 19mm wardrobe & shutter boards
  • Zero core-gap guarantee

04 · BOQ & Value-Engineering Desk

Send us your tender BOQ or interior drawing package. Our specification engineers map out optimal thickness combinations without compromising visual luxury.

  • 1.25mm hero zones + 0.8mm/liner balancing
  • Cross-brand shade equivalence mapping
  • Itemized wastage & sheet-count optimization

05 · Staged Project & Batch Locking

For multi-floor commercial fit-outs and hospitality towers, we lock single-batch dye-lots in our warehouse and release deliveries floor-by-floor on your schedule.

  • Zero shade variation across project phases
  • Moisture-sealed palletized packaging
  • Pan-India dedicated fleet & container loads
View Bulk Slabs

06 · Fabrication Care & Warranty Support

Comprehensive technical guidance on PUR/D3 adhesives, pressing temperatures, protective peel-coat handling, and warranty documentation for end clients.

  • IS:710 BWP borer & termite warranty certificates
  • Acrylic & HPL fabrication advisory sheets
  • Rapid replacement on transit-damaged sheets
